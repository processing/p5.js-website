Move in front of your webcam to shape a colorful field. The ml5.js BodyPose model
tracks your body, and the midpoints of its skeleton connections are passed to a
p5.strands filter shader using a storage buffer. The shader colors each pixel
based on its distance to the nearest point. Pose smoothing keeps the movement fluid.

This example requires a browser with WebGPU support and access to your webcam.
Allow camera access when prompted, and wait for the body tracking model to load.
